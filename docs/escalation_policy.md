# Incident Escalation Policy

The escalation process depends on incident severity and duration.

## Low Severity

Low severity incidents should be handled by the normal operations team.

Escalation is not required unless the incident remains unresolved for more than 8 hours.

## Medium Severity

Medium severity incidents should be escalated if they remain unresolved for more than 2 hours.

The operations supervisor must be notified.

## High Severity

High severity incidents must be reported immediately to the operations supervisor.

If the incident remains unresolved for more than 30 minutes, the incident must also be escalated to the technical manager.

## Critical Severity

Critical incidents must be escalated immediately.

The following teams must be notified:

- operations supervisor
- technical manager
- service manager

Critical incidents affecting passenger safety must receive the highest operational priority.
