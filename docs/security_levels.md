# Incident Severity Levels

The operations team classifies incidents into four severity levels.

## Low

Low severity incidents have limited operational impact.

Examples:

- escalator unavailable
- minor passenger information issues
- non-critical maintenance tasks

Low severity incidents do not require immediate escalation.

## Medium

Medium severity incidents affect service quality but do not stop operations.

Examples:

- delays between 10 and 30 minutes
- temporary overcrowding
- partial equipment failures

Medium incidents should be monitored by the operations team.

## High

High severity incidents significantly affect operations or passenger safety.

Examples:

- train door failures
- signalling degradation
- security incidents
- delays greater than 30 minutes

High severity incidents require notification to the operations supervisor.

## Critical

Critical incidents cause major service interruption or present an immediate safety risk.

Examples:

- complete signalling failure
- power failure affecting an entire station
- major security events
- complete service interruption

Critical incidents require immediate escalation.
