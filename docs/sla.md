# Incident Resolution SLA

The company defines target resolution times according to incident severity.

| Severity | Target Resolution Time |
|----------|------------------------|
| Low | 8 hours |
| Medium | 2 hours |
| High | 1 hour |
| Critical | 30 minutes |

Incidents exceeding their target resolution time are considered SLA violations.

Open incidents must be periodically reviewed.

Critical and high severity incidents should be prioritized over medium and low severity incidents.
